How it works

  1. 1
    It lives on your screenThe game runs in a browser on your café TV, or on tablets on your guest Wi-Fi. When nobody's playing, the screen shows a gentle idle animation instead of going blank.
  2. 2
    Customers scan inThey scan the QR code on the TV and their phone becomes the controller. No app, no sign-up. Play solo, or join the people at the next table.
  3. 3
    The TV keeps scoreLeaderboards run on the TV and rewards land on phones. It's a quick game with a latte, and a reason to come back to beat the top score.

What's on the screen

  • Always on

    A gentle idle animation fills the screen between games. It's never blank and never loud.

  • Solo or together

    One customer can play alone, or a whole table can join the same game from their phones.

  • Leaderboards

    Top scores of the day and week go up on the TV, where everyone can see them.

  • Your menu stays the star

    Board games can wrap around the border of your menu screen, so the menu is still front and centre.

  • Feedback built in

    Every phone controller has a small strip about Zero to Phi and a ? button, so customers can send ideas and bug reports straight to Justin.

  • Table games

    There are also private, phone-only games for friends sharing a table. No TV needed.

Rewards

Land on a special space and get 10% off a coffee. Rewards show up on the player's phone, and you decide what they are and how often they come up. It's a small, friendly reason to stay for one more round and to come back tomorrow.
