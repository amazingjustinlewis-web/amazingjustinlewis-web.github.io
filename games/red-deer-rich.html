Red Deer Rich

Early test 2–8 players TV + phones v0.2 · Game feel Family: Playful (2/5)

A property trading game set in Red Deer, Alberta. Buy deeds, build Shops and Mega-Plexes, make deals on your phone… and when someone lands on your place, hit PAY UP before they pass the dice.

▶ Open on the TV

Early test with placeholder art. Open this on the big screen (TV browser, Chromecast or a PC plugged into the TV). Everyone else scans the QR code with their phone.

PAY UP!

Play it on your TV with phones

  • 1
    Open the game on the TVGo to amazingjustinlewis-web.github.io/games/red-deer-rich/ in the TV's browser (or cast it with DashCast, or use a PC plugged into the TV). A 4-letter room code and a QR code appear.
  • 2
    Join on your phonesScan the QR code, type a name and swipe through the characters. Phones and TV should be on the same Wi-Fi.
  • 3
    StartThe first phone is the host: add computer players, pick house rules and the game length (Regular, Medium or Quick), then tap START GAME.
  • 4
    PlaySwipe up (or tap ROLL), buy or pass, build in My Stuff, and make deals in Deals & Chat. Keep an eye out for the big PAY UP button.
