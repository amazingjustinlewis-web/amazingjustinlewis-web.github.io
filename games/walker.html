Family Friendly / Walker

Walker

First prototype 1 player TV + phone or iPad v0.1 · First stroll

A calm, soft play world, made for Justin’s son Walker. You get six big bright picture buttons. Tap one and you head off to the bouncy castle or the playground. Tap again to go deeper: bounce higher, do a flip, shoot a hoop, swing higher, slide again. Between places there’s a quiet path with birds, a trickle of water, a deer that nods hello, and little moments drifting by that you can stop and watch. Do nothing and it just settles in and people-watches.

▶ Play Walker

On a TV or laptop, scan the QR code with a phone. On an iPad, tap Play here and the six buttons sit along the bottom of the screen.

WALKER

How it works

  • 1
    Open itTV or laptop: scan the QR code with a phone. iPad or tablet: tap Play here (it picks this by itself after a few seconds).
  • 2
    Pick a placeThe green Go page has the castle, playground, rocket, pond, bench and your buddy. Tap fast and you run there.
  • 3
    Tap again to go deeperEach place has its own coloured page with six things to do. More taps make it bigger and bouncier; other kids come over to play when you’re busy.
  • 4
    Or just chillLeave it alone and the view softly settles and watches the world go by. The back arrow walks you out.
