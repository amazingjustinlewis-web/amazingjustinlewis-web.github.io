Family Friendly / Drift Signal

Drift Signal

First prototype 1–6 crew TV + phones v0.2 · Barrel rolls & dogfights

The most chill game ever, where the craziest things happen. The TV is the bridge window of your ship; every phone is a crew station. Tap a destination on the star map, pick a warp speed and the ship computer flies you there. Fly through a comet’s tail and watch the shields light up, send a probe into a black hole and listen to it dissolve into static, or land on a quiet ocean world where something tall rises out of the water… and stares.

▶ Open on the TV

Open this on the big screen (TV browser, Chromecast via DashCast, or a laptop plugged into the TV). Everyone else scans the QR code etched into the bulkhead.

DRIFT

Play it on your TV with phones

  • 1
    Open the bridge on the TVGo to amazingjustinlewis-web.github.io/games/space/ on the TV or a laptop plugged into it, or cast it with DashCast.
  • 2
    Come aboardScan the QR code etched into the bulkhead. The first phone is the captain; everyone after gets a station. Scan in or out any time, or tap On the bridge to step away.
  • 3
    Set a courseOn HELM, tap a destination on the star map (or open a scan category), slide the warp speed and tap Set course. Warp 2 takes hours of ship time; warp 9.5 takes minutes.
  • 4
    DriftScan things, launch probes, reroute power in Engineering, answer aliens from your phones. Land on Thalassa-4471: match orbit, align the corridor, pick a site.
