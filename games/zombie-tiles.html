Zombie Tiles

Prototype Working title 1–4 players TV + phones v0.5 · Teams & Takeoff Family: Intense (4/5)

A zombie board game for your living room. Explore the city one tile at a time, fight with dice, and race to the hidden helipad… where the guard at each gate says YES or NO.

▶ Open on the TV

Open this on the big screen (TV browser or a PC plugged into the TV). Everyone else scans the QR code with their phone. No phones? Add hot-seat players and use the keyboard.

FIND THE HELIPAD

Play it on your TV with phones

  • 1
    Open the game on the TVIn the TV's web browser (for example on a Chromecast with Google TV), or on a PC connected to the TV, go to amazingjustinlewis-web.github.io/games/zombie-tiles/. A 4-letter room code and a QR code appear.
  • 2
    Join on your phonesEveryone scans the QR code (or opens the controller page and types the code), enters a name and picks a pair of dice. Phones and TV should be on the same Wi-Fi.
  • 3
    StartThe first player to join taps START GAME on their phone.
  • 4
    PlayOn your turn: ROLL, tap the arrows to plan a path (the TV shows it), then EXECUTE. Phone fell asleep? Just reopen the page and you're back in your seat.

Explore. Fight. Escape.

  • A new city every game

    The board starts as one crossroads. Walk off an open edge and a new tile is drawn: streets, parks, ponds, parking lots, shops and apartments. You rotate it and attach it.

  • Plan, then go

    Roll two dice, plan your path square by square on your phone while the TV highlights it, then hit EXECUTE and watch your token walk it.

  • Dice fights

    Step next to a zombie and it's a fight: your two dice plus your weapon against the zombie's two dice. Win big for a clean kill; tie and you both get hurt.

  • Pipes, pistols, machine guns

    Pick up weapons, hearts and ammo along the way. A lead pipe adds +2 to your fight roll, a pistol +3 and a machine gun +5 (guns use ammo). Everyone sees what you're holding, but only your phone knows how much ammo is left.

  • The helipad

    Hidden in the back half of the deck: a fortified helipad ringed by zombies. Each of its four gates secretly says YES or NO. First out wins.

  • Down, not out

    Run out of hearts and you're left for dead… then you rise as a zombie and keep playing, hunting your friends. Or just watch.

  • Teams and the chopper

    New in v0.5: play in 2–4 colour-coded teams (teammates never fight each other), wait on the helipad for your friends while the zombies swarm the fence, then take off together. You can also plan a path with drops along the way: two steps, drop TNT, two more steps, go.

  • Traps and dynamite

    New in v0.4: pick up trap boxes and dynamite and drop them from your phone. A zombie that walks into a trap gets caught in a cartoon rope snare. Dynamite goes off at the end of the round (or when you hit DETONATE) and clears the zombies around it… just don't stand too close. Everyone is now a little animated character walking square by square.

  • Computer players

    New in v0.3: short on players? Add AI survivors with their own personality and an Easy, Normal or Ruthless setting. You can see where they plan to go, they chatter in speech bubbles, and one can cover for a phone that drops out. Kids can switch on coach hints on their phone.

  • Lights on (optional)

    New in v0.2: got Philips Hue? Your rooms glow in the current player's colour, flicker on dice rolls, pulse red in fights and flash gold when the chopper takes someone. Your lights go back to normal when the game ends.
