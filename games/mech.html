Family Friendly / Iron Stride

Iron Stride

First prototype 1–2 players TV + phones v0.3 · Trackpad + lock-on Family: Lively (3/5)

You're sitting inside a walking mech. Your phone is the stick: swing it to turn the torso, tilt it to aim, hold FIRE, switch between the autocannon, rockets and a charged rail shot, and draw a path on the map so the legs stomp you there. Cartoon drones and tanks come in from the sides; the mech bay rearms and repairs you.

▶ Open on the TV

First prototype with simple low-poly proxy art. Open this on the big screen (TV browser, Chromecast or a PC plugged into the TV). Everyone else scans the QR code with their phone.

STOMP!

Play it on your TV with phones

  • 1
    Open the game on the TVGo to amazingjustinlewis-web.github.io/games/mech/ on the TV, a laptop plugged into it, or cast it with DashCast. A room code and a QR code appear. No phone handy? Click Play here with mouse + keys.
  • 2
    Climb in with your phoneScan the QR code. The first phone is the pilot; a second phone can join as the gunner with its own crosshair. On an iPhone, tap Tap to enable motion.
  • 3
    Calibrate in three tapsPoint the top of the phone at the centre of the TV, then its left edge, then its right edge. Re-centre any time.
  • 4
    Stomp aroundPush the crosshair to the edge of the TV to turn. Drag the thumb pad to walk (tap it to fire); drag the outer ring arrow to swing the torso. On the MAP tab, tap to walk there or draw a path with your finger. Walk into the blue mech bay to rearm and repair. BRACE soaks hits; hold EJECT for fun.
