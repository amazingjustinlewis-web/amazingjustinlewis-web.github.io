Charge Hop

Early prototype 1–2 players PC keyboard Free · browser v3 · The Lava Run Family: Lively (3/5)

One button does everything. Tap to hop, hold to charge a bigger jump… and don't hold it too long.

▶ Play now

Needs a keyboard for now (phone touch controls are coming). Player 1 is Space, Player 2 is Enter. Use your browser's Back button to return here.

PRESS SPACE

One button. Charge it. Don't blow it.

  • Tap, hold, overload

    Tap to hop. Hold to charge a bigger jump: your robot shakes, glows and catches fire. Hold too long and it overloads, flinging you forward, dizzy.

  • Logs & roads

    Ride drifting logs that bump into each other, so your landing spot keeps moving. Cross busy roads without getting flattened.

  • Domino chains

    Smash through rows of dominoes and set off huge chain reactions that topple across the field.

  • Lava chutes

    Surf rock chunks down lava chutes that speed up and spread apart, then launch you into wide-open sprints.

  • Slow-mo close calls

    Near misses trigger cinematic slow-motion zooms, so every narrow escape looks like a movie moment.

  • 2 players, 1 keyboard

    Race a friend on the same keyboard: Space vs Enter. First to the goal wins, and the winner gets fireworks.

The cast

Player 1 is a chunky yellow toy robot; Player 2 is an orange robot dog. Both are inspired by Benevolence, the student film Justin made at Vancouver Film School.
