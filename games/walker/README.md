# Walker (v0.3)
Calm first-person play world for Walker. `index.html` = TV/iPad (QR + "Play here"), `controller.html` = phone pad (`?room=CODE`, `?local=1` for same-browser tests). v0.1 and v0.2 are archived in `v0.1/` and `v0.2/` (own PeerJS prefixes `ztp-walker-v01-`, `ztp-walker-v02a-`; v0.3 uses `ztp-walker-v03-`).
- v0.3: drawn SVG icons (swing, seesaw, merry-go-round, sandcastle, water slide), no Home tab / Back button, swing+seesaw camera in sync, seesaw partner, dome walk-climb-top, sandbox castle/bucket/pat cycle, jittered basketball (~8/10) with backboard light + fireworks, wave hand, boat motor, coaster lap bar + stay-on + look-toward-and-go-after, bigger iPad tabs, slide poles outside the helix. Debug: `jit=` shot wobble.
- Pages/buttons/moments/settings defaults: `js/config.js`. Home page = six categories (Bouncy, Playground, Petting zoo, Water park, Rides, Quiet), each opens six activities. Pad UI shared by phone and Play here: `js/ui.js` + `js/pad.css`.
- `js/world.js` builds the park; `js/world2.js` adds the wooden coaster (NW), petting zoo (NE), water park (SW), bumper boat lagoon (SE) and their idle life. `js/tv.js` runs camera/activities/kids/buddy/weather/Hue stub, `js/sfx.js` synth sounds + speechSynthesis.
- Tap-to-run: travel speed follows tap rate (walk 1.8 m/s, up to ~9), eases back to a stroll. Car toggle: Rides page or settings (6 m/s base).
- Test harness: `/workspace/walker-tests/sim.py` (serve repo on :8765). Shots in `shots/` (`v03-*` for this version).
- TV debug params: `q=0-4` quality, `mute`, `weather=sun|cloud|rain|bow`, `auto=here`, `ff=N` speed, `local=1&room=TEST`.
