Family Friendly / Laser Range

Laser Range

Early test 1–4 players TV + phones v0.1 · First test Family: Lively (3/5)

Your phone is a light gun. Point it at the TV and your crosshair follows. Pop the cartoon targets that duck behind barrels, crates and walls, blow up the red barrels for big chain reactions… and don’t shoot granny.

▶ Open on the TV

Early test with placeholder art. Open this on the big screen (TV browser, Chromecast or a PC plugged into the TV). Everyone else scans the QR code with their phone.

ZAP!

Play it on your TV with phones

  • 1
    Open the game on the TVGo to amazingjustinlewis-web.github.io/games/laser-range/ in the TV's browser (or cast it with DashCast, or use a PC plugged into the TV). A 4-letter room code and a QR code appear.
  • 2
    Join on your phonesScan the QR code and type a name. On an iPhone, tap Tap to enable aiming. No motion sensor? Use the touchpad instead. Phones and TV should be on the same Wi-Fi.
  • 3
    CalibrateFive targets in your colour appear on the TV. Point the top of your phone at the one your phone names and tap. Cursor drifting later? Tap Re-centre.
  • 4
    Shoot!The first phone taps START. Tap SHOOT to fire, swipe to throw a blast, and shoot the balloon to get the charge cannon (hold SHOOT, then let go). 90 seconds, then the results.
