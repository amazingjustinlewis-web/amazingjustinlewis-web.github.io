IRON STRIDE

working title

You're inside a walking mech. Your phone is the stick: swing it to turn, tilt it to aim, draw a path on the map and the legs do the walking.

  • 1 Scan the code with your phone (the first phone is the pilot, a second one can join as gunner).
  • 2 Point the top of your phone at the TV and do the 3-tap calibration.
  • 3 Tap START. Hold FIRE, switch guns, tap the map to walk, walk into the bay to rearm.
On a phone? This is the TV screen. Open it on the TV or a laptop, then join with the phone controller.
