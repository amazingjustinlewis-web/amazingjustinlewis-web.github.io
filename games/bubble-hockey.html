How it works

  • 1
    Open it on a TV or big screenOpen the game on a TV, a computer plugged into a TV, or a laptop. A room code and QR code appear.
  • 2
    Join on your phonesScan the QR code. Phones and the screen should share the same Wi-Fi. Each phone is a set of rods.
  • 3
    PlayDrag to move, dangle and flick to pass or shoot. Things are still being tuned.
