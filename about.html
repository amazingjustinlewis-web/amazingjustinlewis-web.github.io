PLAYER PROFILE

Justin Lewis

Zero to Phi, founded by Justin Lewis, makes emergent, connected, visceral games: one simple connection point, lots of freedom inside the system, and mathematically clean rails underneath. The name runs from 0 to φ, the golden ratio.

Justin is a professional character animator based in Red Deer, Alberta, with credits on the feature films 9 and Hoodwinked. Along the way he has worked at Starz Animation, Silicon Knights and Nerd Corps.

He graduated from Vancouver Film School, where his student film Benevolence got him recruited onto 9. The robots in his first game, Charge Hop, are inspired by that film.

He's also a punk rock drummer who makes music.

This site is where Zero to Phi's indie games live. Charge Hop is the first; plenty more are planned.

Some of these will one day spin up in the GyroForge, Justin's patented gyro ride pod.
